# Running an HSK 1 mock sitting

This is a reader-administered, offline practice sitting. It follows the current regular HSK 1 (HSK 2.0) section order and timing, but it is **not an official HSK test** and cannot certify a level. The candidate booklet, a blank answer sheet, a pencil and a quiet room are enough. Keep `answers.md`/`answers.pdf` and `examiner-listening.md`/`examiner-listening.pdf` away from the candidate.

## Materials and roles

- **Candidate:** one form's `booklet.pdf` and `answer-sheet.pdf`; a pencil and eraser; no dictionary, notes, phone, smart watch or other device.
- **Examiner/reader:** this file, the form's `examiner-listening.pdf`, a clock, and the private `answers.pdf` only after the sitting. The reader must be a fluent Mandarin speaker who can read the script at a clear, natural beginner pace.
- **Room:** quiet, with a visible clock if possible. Seat candidates so they cannot see each other's answer sheets. Do not display the examiner script or the key.

## Timing

| Stage | Time | Notes |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Personal information | 5 min | Name, candidate number, date; keep this outside the 35 minutes of test content. |
| Listening instructions and examples | about 2 min | Read the part instruction once; read the example once or twice as marked. |
| Listening Part 1 (1–5) | about 2.5 min | Each phrase twice; 5 s after the first reading, 10 s after the second. |
| Listening Part 2 (6–10) | about 2.5 min | Each sentence twice; same pauses. |
| Listening Part 3 (11–15) | about 4 min | Each two-turn dialogue twice; same pauses. |
| Listening Part 4 (16–20) | about 4 min | Statement, question and three options twice; same pauses. |
| Transfer listening answers | 3 min | Stop the audio/reader; candidates transfer 1–20 to the answer sheet. |
| Reading (21–40) | 17 min | Four parts; no reader. |
| Collect | — | Collect booklets and answer sheets before review. |

The total is about 40 minutes including the 5 minutes for personal information. The listening script is written to finish in 12–15 minutes; do not pad it with long silences to imitate a recording, and do not shorten the reading time to compensate.

## Listening protocol

1. Read the part instruction once in Chinese, then once in English if the group needs it. Do not translate the scored items.
2. Read each example as marked, then give the example answer printed in the booklet.
3. Read every scored item **twice**. Pause about 5 seconds after the first reading and about 10 seconds after the second, or slightly longer if the group is large. Keep the same pace for the whole section.
4. Part 1: read the phrase only. Do not describe the picture.
5. Part 2: read the sentence only. Do not read the three picture labels or describe them.
6. Part 3: read the two turns with a short natural gap. Do not announce “男/女” as a label; use a distinct voice or a short pause. Read the dialogue twice.
7. Part 4: read the statement, the question and the three options in order, then repeat the whole item. Do not add the letter after an option.
8. Do not repeat an item a third time, correct a candidate, answer questions about meaning, or reveal whether an answer is right. If a candidate asks, say that the item has been read twice and the sitting continues.
9. At the end, read the closing line and allow 3 minutes to transfer answers. Then start the Reading section.

If the reader makes a substantive error (wrong item, omitted option, clearly wrong tone/word), record the item number and the error. Do not silently adjust the candidate's score; either exclude the affected item from the raw denominator and say so, or void and rerun the listening section if the error affected several items. Do not claim that a spoiled delivery was valid.

## Reading protocol

- Give the full 17 minutes. Candidates may work through the four parts in any order within the section; they may not return to the listening section.
- The booklet contains the pictures and printed words/options. No reader is needed.
- Part 1: mark √ or × for each picture–word pair.
- Part 2: write the letter of the matching picture; one picture is not used.
- Part 3: write the letter of the matching answer; one answer is not used.
- Part 4: write the letter of the word that fills the blank; one option is not used.
- At the end, stop writing and collect the answer sheet. Do not let candidates change answers while papers are being collected.

## Marking

Use the form's `answers.pdf` or `answers.md`. One mark per correct answer; no mark for a wrong or blank answer; no negative marking.

| Section | Marks |
| --- | ---: |
| Listening 1–20 | 20 |
| Reading 21–40 | 20 |
| **Raw total** | **40** |

For a practice score out of 200, multiply each section's correct count by 5 and add the two sections. The usual HSK 1 pass mark is 120/200. This is a raw practice conversion, not an official equated HSK score; do not issue a certificate or claim an official level.

Record the section scores separately, for example `Listening 16/20; Reading 15/20; Raw 31/40; Practice 155/200`. The example is notation only, not a candidate result.

## Review

- Return the booklet and answer sheet after marking, or review in a separate session. Give the candidate the key only after the sitting.
- For listening errors, replay the item by reading the transcript in `answers.md`/`answers.pdf`. The transcript is also the only place where the full dialogue/statement appears.
- For picture items, point to the picture the key names and ask the candidate to say what they saw; this catches picture ambiguity as well as vocabulary errors.
- The examples are not scored; do not include them in the total.
- If an item is genuinely ambiguous, mark it for the pack owner rather than silently accepting two answers.

## Accommodations and limits

- Extra time or a separate room may be arranged for practice, but it changes the timed conditions; record it. Do not describe an accommodated sitting as an official-timing result.
- There is no audio file, so a recorded-pacing claim cannot be made. A reader session is a valid offline adaptation, not a reproduction of the official recording.
- A qualified Chinese teacher has not reviewed these forms. Treat all keys and explanations as drafts until teacher review is recorded.
- The pack does not cover HSK 3.0. Check the candidate's registration format before using it; see [SOURCES.md](SOURCES.md).
