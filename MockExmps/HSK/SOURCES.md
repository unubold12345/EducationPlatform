# Official reference record

Checked on 2026-11-10. These references were used to confirm the **format** and the **HSK 1 vocabulary range** only. No official HSK question, answer, recording, picture or answer key is bundled or reproduced. The pack is original practice content and is not affiliated with or approved by the test operators.

## Format and timing

| Source | What it was used for |
| --- | --- |
| [Chinese Testing International — HSK Level 1](https://www.chinesetest.cn/HSK/1) | Current official test page; the entry point for the HSK 1 structure and vocabulary links. |
| [Official HSK 1 sample paper](https://www.chinesetest.cn/userfiles/file/HSK1.pdf) | Layout and task sequence. The sample confirms 40 items, Listening 20 / about 15 minutes, 3 minutes to transfer listening answers, and about 40 minutes including 5 minutes for personal information. Its cover prints 15 minutes for Reading, while the current official section table gives 17; this pack uses the current 17-minute table. The sample also confirms the picture tasks in Listening Parts 1–3 and Reading Parts 1–2. The PDF itself is not included in this pack. |
| [Zhejiang Normal University Confucius Institute — HSK 1 introduction](https://ci.zjnu.edu.cn/wwwyjjs/main.psp) | A second public copy of the official HSK 1 section table and item descriptions, used to cross-check the part counts and task wording. |
| [HSK Crestar Singapore — HSK 1](https://hsk-crestar.com.sg/zh-hans/hsk-level-1/) | A test-centre summary of the same section table and task descriptions, used as a third check on the counts. |

The official sample’s picture usage is the reason this pack supplies pictures for all five picture-based parts. The drawings are new; no sample picture is traced, copied or bundled.

## Vocabulary

| Source | What it was used for |
| --- | --- |
| [HSK vocabulary reference (2015 syllabus)](https://www.unige.ch/ic/application/files/7915/4272/2792/HSK_VOCABULARY_.pdf) | The 150-word HSK 1 syllabus list used by the validator. The reference PDF’s numbering omits entry 62; 没 is treated as the omitted item, in line with the standard list and the sample paper’s 没有. |
| [Official HSK 1 sample paper](https://www.chinesetest.cn/userfiles/file/HSK1.pdf) | Confirms that the official paper itself uses 火车 and 饭馆 and a picture of a cat for 猫, although the 150-word reference list does not print those entries separately. The pack therefore allows 火车, 饭馆 and 猫 in addition to the 150 syllabus words. Proper names and speaker labels are not vocabulary items. |

The validator segments every Chinese candidate-facing string against this list. A pass means no character string fell outside the list; it does not prove that a sentence is natural or that the grammar is exactly HSK 1. Teacher review is still required.

## HSK 2.0 versus HSK 3.0

| Source | What it was used for |
| --- | --- |
| [HSK 3.0 versus the current HSK (2026 guide)](https://hskmock.com/en/blog/370.html) | Secondary summary checked on 2026-11-10: regular HSK 1–6 dates in 2026 continue to use the current six-level (HSK 2.0) format; HSK 3.0 is being introduced through specifically announced sessions, including a 20 September 2026 trial. |
| [Chinese Testing International — notices](https://www.chinesetest.cn/notice) | Official notice index for the HSK 3.0 rollout; the exact session list and registration rules should be checked there or with the test centre before registering. |

The pack is deliberately built for the **current regular HSK 1**, not HSK 3.0. HSK 3.0 Level 1 has a larger vocabulary target (about 300 words) and a revised picture/text mix. Do not use these forms as an HSK 3.0 rehearsal without checking the session’s syllabus.

## Audio

The official sample audio is linked from the official page as `level-1.wma`. It was **not** downloaded, transformed or bundled. This pack has no audio; the listening section is read by an examiner. See [ADMINISTRATION.md](ADMINISTRATION.md). No synthetic recording is presented as official pacing.
