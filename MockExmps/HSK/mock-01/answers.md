## Listening

### Part 1 (1–5)

**1. √** — 坐出租车。 *Zuò chūzūchē.*  
The phrase says “take a taxi”; the picture shows a taxi.

**2. ×** — 下雨了。 *Xià yǔ le.*  
The phrase says it is raining, but the picture shows a sunny day.

**3. √** — 看电视。 *Kàn diànshì.*  
The phrase says “watch TV”; the picture shows a television.

**4. ×** — 妈妈吃米饭。 *Māma chī mǐfàn.*  
The phrase says mother is eating rice, but she is reading a book.

**5. √** — 喝茶。 *Hē chá.*  
The phrase says “drink tea”; the picture shows a cup of tea.

### Part 2 (6–10)

**6. B** — 我妈妈喜欢喝茶。 *Wǒ māma xǐhuan hē chá.*  
Options: A An apple　B A cup of tea　C A bowl of rice  
The sentence says my mother likes drinking tea; B shows tea.

**7. A** — 今天下雨了。 *Jīntiān xià yǔ le.*  
Options: A A person in the rain　B A sunny day　C A person dressed for cold weather  
The sentence says it is raining today; A shows rain.

**8. C** — 他坐飞机去北京。 *Tā zuò fēijī qù Běijīng.*  
Options: A A train　B A taxi　C A passenger plane  
“坐飞机” means “take a plane”; C shows a plane.

**9. B** — 女儿在睡觉。 *Nǚ'ér zài shuìjiào.*  
Options: A A person reading　B A person sleeping in bed　C A person eating  
The sentence says the daughter is sleeping; B shows someone sleeping.

**10. A** — 我们中午吃米饭。 *Wǒmen zhōngwǔ chī mǐfàn.*  
Options: A A bowl of rice　B A bowl of fruit　C A glass of water  
The sentence says we eat rice at noon; A shows a bowl of rice.

### Part 3 (11–15) — transcript

**11. A**  
女：喂，你在哪儿？ *Wèi, nǐ zài nǎr?*  
男：我在家。 *Wǒ zài jiā.*  
They are talking on the phone; A shows a phone call.

**12. B**  
男：这个杯子多少钱？ *Zhège bēizi duōshao qián?*  
女：十块钱。 *Shí kuài qián.*  
They are asking about a cup's price in a shop; B shows a shop.

**13. C**  
女：你怎么了？ *Nǐ zěnme le?*  
男：我很冷。 *Wǒ hěn lěng.*  
The woman asks how he feels and he says he is cold; C shows a doctor and patient.

**14. D**  
男：你想吃什么？ *Nǐ xiǎng chī shénme?*  
女：我想吃米饭。 *Wǒ xiǎng chī mǐfàn.*  
They are ordering food in a restaurant; D shows a restaurant.

**15. E**  
女：今天天气怎么样？ *Jīntiān tiānqì zěnmeyàng?*  
男：下雨了。 *Xià yǔ le.*  
The man says it is raining; E shows rain.

### Part 4 (16–20) — transcript

**16. B**  
明天是三月五号，星期三。 *Míngtiān shì sān yuè wǔ hào, xīngqīsān.*  
问：明天是星期几？ *Míngtiān shì xīngqī jǐ?*  
A 星期一 *xīngqīyī*　B 星期三 *xīngqīsān*　C 星期五 *xīngqīwǔ*  
The statement says tomorrow is Wednesday; B is 星期三.

**17. C**  
我坐火车去北京。 *Wǒ zuò huǒchē qù Běijīng.*  
问：他怎么去北京？ *Tā zěnme qù Běijīng?*  
A 坐飞机 *zuò fēijī*　B 坐出租车 *zuò chūzūchē*　C 坐火车 *zuò huǒchē*  
The speaker says “坐火车” (take a train); C is 坐火车.

**18. A**  
我妈妈是老师，她在学校工作。 *Wǒ māma shì lǎoshī, tā zài xuéxiào gōngzuò.*  
问：他妈妈在哪儿工作？ *Tā māma zài nǎr gōngzuò?*  
A 学校 *xuéxiào*　B 医院 *yīyuàn*　C 商店 *shāngdiàn*  
The statement says she works at a school; A is 学校.

**19. C**  
我有一个女儿，她五岁。 *Wǒ yǒu yí gè nǚ'ér, tā wǔ suì.*  
问：他女儿几岁？ *Tā nǚ'ér jǐ suì?*  
A 四岁 *sì suì*　B 六岁 *liù suì*　C 五岁 *wǔ suì*  
The daughter is five years old; C is 五岁.

**20. B**  
昨天天气很热，今天下雨了。 *Zuótiān tiānqì hěn rè, jīntiān xià yǔ le.*  
问：今天天气怎么样？ *Jīntiān tiānqì zěnmeyàng?*  
A 很热 *hěn rè*　B 下雨 *xià yǔ*　C 很冷 *hěn lěng*  
Today it is raining; B is 下雨.

## Reading

### Part 1 (21–25)

**21. √** — 火车 *huǒchē*  
The picture shows a train; 火车 means “train”.

**22. ×** — 猫 *māo*  
The picture shows a dog, not a cat (猫).

**23. √** — 苹果 *píngguǒ*  
The picture shows an apple; 苹果 means “apple”.

**24. ×** — 电视 *diànshì*  
The picture shows a computer (电脑), not a television (电视).

**25. √** — 睡觉 *shuìjiào*  
The picture shows someone sleeping; 睡觉 means “sleep”.

### Part 2 (26–30)

**26. C** — 他坐出租车去学校。 *Tā zuò chūzūchē qù xuéxiào.*  
“坐出租车” matches C, a taxi.

**27. D** — 她在饭馆吃米饭。 *Tā zài fànguǎn chī mǐfàn.*  
饭馆 means “restaurant”; D shows a restaurant.

**28. E** — 我下午在家看电视。 *Wǒ xiàwǔ zài jiā kàn diànshì.*  
The sentence mentions watching TV at home; E shows a television at home.

**29. A** — 我妈妈是医生，她在医院工作。 *Wǒ māma shì yīshēng, tā zài yīyuàn gōngzuò.*  
The sentence describes a doctor; A shows a doctor.

**30. F** — 昨天下雨了，天气很冷。 *Zuótiān xià yǔ le, tiānqì hěn lěng.*  
The sentence says it rained yesterday and was cold; F shows rain.

### Part 3 (31–35)

**31. B** — 你叫什么名字？ *Nǐ jiào shénme míngzi?*  
→ 我叫王月。 *Wǒ jiào Wáng Yuè.*  
The question asks for a name; B gives the name Wang Yue.

**32. C** — 现在几点？ *Xiànzài jǐ diǎn?*  
→ 现在三点。 *Xiànzài sān diǎn.*  
The question asks the time; C says it is three o'clock.

**33. A** — 你几岁？ *Nǐ jǐ suì?*  
→ 我八岁。 *Wǒ bā suì.*  
The question asks an age; A says “I am eight”.

**34. E** — 你喜欢什么水果？ *Nǐ xǐhuan shénme shuǐguǒ?*  
→ 我喜欢苹果。 *Wǒ xǐhuan píngguǒ.*  
The question asks about fruit; E says “I like apples”.

**35. D** — 你怎么去北京？ *Nǐ zěnme qù Běijīng?*  
→ 我坐火车去。 *Wǒ zuò huǒchē qù.*  
The question asks how; D says “I go by train”.

### Part 4 (36–40)

**36. D** — 你写（　）吗？  
→ 名字 *míngzi*  
“你写名字吗？” asks whether you write your name; D is 名字.

**37. B** — 你家有（　）个人？  
→ 几 *jǐ*  
“你家有几个人？” asks how many people; B is 几.

**38. A** — 我上午（　）学校。  
→ 在 *zài*  
“我上午在学校” needs the location marker 在; A.

**39. C** — 女：（　），我不能去。 男：没关系。  
→ 对不起 *duìbuqǐ*  
The woman apologises, so 对不起 fits; C.

**40. F** — 明天我们（　）汉语。  
→ 学习 *xuéxí*  
“学习汉语” means study Chinese; F is 学习.

## Scoring

The raw practice score is **5 × correct answers in each section** out of 200, and the usual HSK 1 pass mark is 120/200. This conversion is for practice only; it is not an official equated HSK score and does not certify a level.
