# HSK 1 Mock 01 — examiner's listening script

*在学校和家里 · Zài xuéxiào hé jiā li*

**Private examiner copy.** The candidate booklet does not print the listening texts. Read at a clear, natural beginner pace. Read every example and every item **twice**. Do not translate, add clues, or read the answer key.

## Timing

| Stage | Suggested time |
| --- | ---: |
| Instructions and example | 2 minutes |
| Part 1 (1–5) | about 2.5 minutes |
| Part 2 (6–10) | about 2.5 minutes |
| Part 3 (11–15) | about 4 minutes |
| Part 4 (16–20) | about 4 minutes |
| Transfer answers | 3 minutes |

The exact reading speed varies. The official listening section is about 15 minutes; this script is designed to finish in 12–15 minutes with the pauses below. Do not add long silent waits to fill the official window.

## Before Part 1

“HSK 一级模拟考试，听力现在开始。” *HSK yījí mónǐ kǎoshì, tīnglì xiànzài kāishǐ.* Pause 5 seconds. Read the instructions for each part once, then begin.

## Part 3 (11–15)

Instruction: “第三部分，一共五个题，每题听两次。听录音，选择相应的图片。” *Dì-sān bùfen, yígòng wǔ gè tí, měi tí tīng liǎng cì. Tīng lùyīn, xuǎnzé xiāngyìng de túpiàn.*

### Example

女：你好！ *Nǐ hǎo!*  
男：你好！很高兴认识你。 *Nǐ hǎo! Hěn gāoxìng rènshi nǐ.* — read twice. The booklet shows answer A.

## Part 4 (16–20)

Instruction: “第四部分，一共五个题，每题听两次。听录音，选择答案。” *Dì-sì bùfen, yígòng wǔ gè tí, měi tí tīng liǎng cì. Tīng lùyīn, xuǎnzé dá'àn.*

### Example

“下午我去商店，我想买一些水果。问：她去哪儿？ A 商店 B 医院 C 学校。” *Xiàwǔ wǒ qù shāngdiàn, wǒ xiǎng mǎi yìxiē shuǐguǒ. Wèn: Tā qù nǎr? A shāngdiàn B yīyuàn C xuéxiào.* — read twice. The booklet shows answer A.

### 16

“明天是三月五号，星期三。 问：明天是星期几？ A 星期一 B 星期三 C 星期五。”  
*Míngtiān shì sān yuè wǔ hào, xīngqīsān. Wèn: Míngtiān shì xīngqī jǐ? A xīngqīyī B xīngqīsān C xīngqīwǔ.*  
Read the statement, question and three options twice. Pause 5 seconds after the first reading and 10 seconds after the second.

### 17

“我坐火车去北京。 问：他怎么去北京？ A 坐飞机 B 坐出租车 C 坐火车。”  
*Wǒ zuò huǒchē qù Běijīng. Wèn: Tā zěnme qù Běijīng? A zuò fēijī B zuò chūzūchē C zuò huǒchē.*  
Read the statement, question and three options twice. Pause 5 seconds after the first reading and 10 seconds after the second.

### 18

“我妈妈是老师，她在学校工作。 问：他妈妈在哪儿工作？ A 学校 B 医院 C 商店。”  
*Wǒ māma shì lǎoshī, tā zài xuéxiào gōngzuò. Wèn: Tā māma zài nǎr gōngzuò? A xuéxiào B yīyuàn C shāngdiàn.*  
Read the statement, question and three options twice. Pause 5 seconds after the first reading and 10 seconds after the second.

### 19

“我有一个女儿，她五岁。 问：他女儿几岁？ A 四岁 B 六岁 C 五岁。”  
*Wǒ yǒu yí gè nǚ'ér, tā wǔ suì. Wèn: Tā nǚ'ér jǐ suì? A sì suì B liù suì C wǔ suì.*  
Read the statement, question and three options twice. Pause 5 seconds after the first reading and 10 seconds after the second.

### 20

“昨天天气很热，今天下雨了。 问：今天天气怎么样？ A 很热 B 下雨 C 很冷。”  
*Zuótiān tiānqì hěn rè, jīntiān xià yǔ le. Wèn: Jīntiān tiānqì zěnmeyàng? A hěn rè B xià yǔ C hěn lěng.*  
Read the statement, question and three options twice. Pause 5 seconds after the first reading and 10 seconds after the second.

## After the listening section

“听力考试现在结束。请把听力答案写到答题卡上。” *Tīnglì kǎoshì xiànzài jiéshù. Qǐng bǎ tīnglì dá'àn xiě dào dátíkǎ shàng.* Allow 3 minutes, then start the Reading section.
