## Listening

### Part 1 (1–5)

**1. ×** — 坐飞机。 *Zuò fēijī.*  
The phrase says “take a plane”, but the picture shows a train.

**2. √** — 喝水。 *Hē shuǐ.*  
The phrase says “drink water”; the picture shows a glass of water.

**3. √** — 很冷。 *Hěn lěng.*  
The phrase says “very cold”; the picture shows a cold day.

**4. ×** — 去医院。 *Qù yīyuàn.*  
The phrase says “go to the hospital”, but the picture shows a school.

**5. √** — 打电话。 *Dǎ diànhuà.*  
The phrase says “make a phone call”; the picture shows someone on the phone.

### Part 2 (6–10)

**6. A** — 我爸爸是医生。 *Wǒ bàba shì yīshēng.*  
Options: A A doctor in a white coat　B A teacher at a blackboard　C A student reading  
The sentence says my father is a doctor; A shows a doctor.

**7. C** — 今天很热。 *Jīntiān hěn rè.*  
Options: A A person dressed for cold weather　B A person in the rain　C A sunny day  
The sentence says it is very hot; C shows a sunny, hot day.

**8. B** — 他坐火车去学校。 *Tā zuò huǒchē qù xuéxiào.*  
Options: A A passenger plane　B A train　C A taxi  
“坐火车” means “take a train”; B shows a train.

**9. A** — 我在商店买水果。 *Wǒ zài shāngdiàn mǎi shuǐguǒ.*  
Options: A A shop counter with fruit　B Two people eating in a restaurant　C A hospital building  
The sentence mentions buying fruit in a shop; A shows a shop.

**10. C** — 她在家看电视。 *Tā zài jiā kàn diànshì.*  
Options: A An open book　B A computer　C A television  
The sentence says she watches TV at home; C shows a television.

### Part 3 (11–15) — transcript

**11. A**  
男：火车几点开？ *Huǒchē jǐ diǎn kāi?*  
女：下午三点。 *Xiàwǔ sān diǎn.*  
They are talking about a train time at a station; A shows a station.

**12. B**  
女：你怎么了？ *Nǐ zěnme le?*  
男：我很热。 *Wǒ hěn rè.*  
The woman asks how he feels and he says he is hot; B shows a doctor and patient.

**13. F**  
女：这个苹果多少钱？ *Zhège píngguǒ duōshao qián?*  
男：五块钱。 *Wǔ kuài qián.*  
They are asking about the price of apples in a shop; F shows a shop.

**14. D**  
男：你在哪儿工作？ *Nǐ zài nǎr gōngzuò?*  
女：我在学校工作。 *Wǒ zài xuéxiào gōngzuò.*  
She says she works at a school; D shows a school.

**15. E**  
女：你在做什么？ *Nǐ zài zuò shénme?*  
男：我在家看电视。 *Wǒ zài jiā kàn diànshì.*  
He says he is watching TV at home; E shows a person watching TV.

### Part 4 (16–20) — transcript

**16. A**  
我上午七点看电视。 *Wǒ shàngwǔ qī diǎn kàn diànshì.*  
问：他上午几点看电视？ *Tā shàngwǔ jǐ diǎn kàn diànshì?*  
A 七点 *qī diǎn*　B 六点 *liù diǎn*　C 八点 *bā diǎn*  
The statement says seven o'clock; A is 七点.

**17. B**  
我坐出租车去火车站。 *Wǒ zuò chūzūchē qù huǒchēzhàn.*  
问：他怎么去火车站？ *Tā zěnme qù huǒchēzhàn?*  
A 坐火车 *zuò huǒchē*　B 坐出租车 *zuò chūzūchē*　C 坐飞机 *zuò fēijī*  
The speaker says “坐出租车”; B is 坐出租车.

**18. C**  
我妈妈是医生，她在医院工作。 *Wǒ māma shì yīshēng, tā zài yīyuàn gōngzuò.*  
问：他妈妈在哪儿工作？ *Tā māma zài nǎr gōngzuò?*  
A 学校 *xuéxiào*　B 商店 *shāngdiàn*　C 医院 *yīyuàn*  
The statement says she works in a hospital; C is 医院.

**19. A**  
我有一个儿子，他四岁。 *Wǒ yǒu yí gè érzi, tā sì suì.*  
问：他儿子几岁？ *Tā érzi jǐ suì?*  
A 四岁 *sì suì*　B 五岁 *wǔ suì*　C 六岁 *liù suì*  
The son is four years old; A is 四岁.

**20. B**  
今天天气太冷了，我不去商店。 *Jīntiān tiānqì tài lěng le, wǒ bú qù shāngdiàn.*  
问：今天天气怎么样？ *Jīntiān tiānqì zěnmeyàng?*  
A 很热 *hěn rè*　B 很冷 *hěn lěng*　C 下雨 *xià yǔ*  
The statement says it is too cold; B is 很冷.

## Reading

### Part 1 (21–25)

**21. √** — 飞机 *fēijī*  
The picture shows a plane; 飞机 means “plane”.

**22. √** — 狗 *gǒu*  
The picture shows a dog; 狗 means “dog”.

**23. ×** — 书 *shū*  
The picture shows a computer (电脑), not a book (书).

**24. √** — 水 *shuǐ*  
The picture shows a glass of water; 水 means “water”.

**25. ×** — 医院 *yīyuàn*  
The picture shows a school (学校), not a hospital (医院).

### Part 2 (26–30)

**26. A** — 妈妈在商店买水果。 *Māma zài shāngdiàn mǎi shuǐguǒ.*  
The sentence mentions a shop and fruit; A shows a shop.

**27. B** — 他是医生。 *Tā shì yīshēng.*  
The sentence says he is a doctor; B shows a doctor.

**28. C** — 学生在学校学习汉语。 *Xuésheng zài xuéxiào xuéxí Hànyǔ.*  
The sentence mentions studying at school; C shows a school.

**29. D** — 今天下雨了，天气很冷。 *Jīntiān xià yǔ le, tiānqì hěn lěng.*  
The sentence says it is raining; D shows rain.

**30. E** — 我们在饭馆吃米饭。 *Wǒmen zài fànguǎn chī mǐfàn.*  
The sentence mentions eating in a restaurant; E shows a restaurant.

### Part 3 (31–35)

**31. C** — 你妈妈在哪儿工作？ *Nǐ māma zài nǎr gōngzuò?*  
→ 她在医院工作。 *Tā zài yīyuàn gōngzuò.*  
The question asks where the mother works; C says in a hospital.

**32. A** — 你多大了？ *Nǐ duō dà le?*  
→ 我七岁。 *Wǒ qī suì.*  
The question asks an age; A says “I am seven”.

**33. D** — 你爱吃什么水果？ *Nǐ ài chī shénme shuǐguǒ?*  
→ 我喜欢苹果。 *Wǒ xǐhuan píngguǒ.*  
The question asks about fruit; D says “I like apples”.

**34. B** — 你家有几个人？ *Nǐ jiā yǒu jǐ gè rén?*  
→ 我家有三个人。 *Wǒ jiā yǒu sān gè rén.*  
The question asks how many people are in the family; B says three.

**35. F** — 现在几点？ *Xiànzài jǐ diǎn?*  
→ 现在下午三点。 *Xiànzài xiàwǔ sān diǎn.*  
The question asks the time; F says three in the afternoon.

### Part 4 (36–40)

**36. C** — 这个杯子（　）吗？  
→ 漂亮 *piàoliang*  
“这个杯子漂亮吗？” asks whether the cup is pretty; C is 漂亮.

**37. A** — （　）是王老师？  
→ 谁 *shéi*  
“谁是王老师？” asks who Teacher Wang is; A is 谁.

**38. F** — 你妈妈在（　）工作。  
→ 医院 *yīyuàn*  
The sentence needs a workplace; F is 医院.

**39. D** — 你有（　）钱？  
→ 多少 *duōshao*  
“你有多少钱？” asks how much money you have; D is 多少.

**40. E** — 我想（　）茶。  
→ 喝 *hē*  
“我想喝茶” needs the verb 喝; E.

This conversion is for practice only; it is not an official equated HSK score and does not certify a level.
