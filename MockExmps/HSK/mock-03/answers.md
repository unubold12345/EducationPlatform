## Listening

### Part 1 (1–5)

**1. ×** — 坐火车。 *Zuò huǒchē.*  
The phrase says “take a train”, but the picture shows a plane.

**2. √** — 一个杯子。 *Yí gè bēizi.*  
The phrase says “a cup”; the picture shows a cup.

**3. √** — 睡觉。 *Shuìjiào.*  
The phrase says “sleep”; the picture shows someone sleeping.

**4. ×** — 在商店买东西。 *Zài shāngdiàn mǎi dōngxi.*  
The phrase says “buy things in a shop”, but the picture shows a hospital.

**5. ×** — 写名字。 *Xiě míngzi.*  
The phrase says “write a name”, but the picture shows someone reading.

### Part 2 (6–10)

**6. B** — 我妈妈在饭馆工作。 *Wǒ māma zài fànguǎn gōngzuò.*  
Options: A A school building　B Two people eating in a restaurant　C A hospital building  
The sentence says my mother works in a restaurant; B shows a restaurant.

**7. A** — 今天很冷。 *Jīntiān hěn lěng.*  
Options: A A person dressed for cold weather　B A sunny day　C A person in the rain  
The sentence says it is very cold; A shows a cold day.

**8. C** — 我们坐出租车去火车站。 *Wǒmen zuò chūzūchē qù huǒchēzhàn.*  
Options: A A train　B A passenger plane　C A taxi  
“坐出租车” means “take a taxi”; C shows a taxi.

**9. A** — 我朋友喜欢小狗。 *Wǒ péngyou xǐhuan xiǎo gǒu.*  
Options: A A small dog　B A cat　C An apple  
The sentence says my friend likes small dogs; A shows a dog.

**10. B** — 她下午在家睡觉。 *Tā xiàwǔ zài jiā shuìjiào.*  
Options: A A person reading　B A person sleeping in bed　C A person writing  
The sentence says she sleeps at home in the afternoon; B shows sleeping.

### Part 3 (11–15) — transcript

**11. C**  
女：火车站在哪儿？ *Huǒchēzhàn zài nǎr?*  
男：在前面。 *Zài qiánmiàn.*  
They are asking where the train station is; C shows a station.

**12. A**  
女：我们怎么去火车站？ *Wǒmen zěnme qù huǒchēzhàn?*  
男：坐出租车去。 *Zuò chūzūchē qù.*  
They decide to take a taxi; A shows a taxi.

**13. B**  
男：你明天怎么去北京？ *Nǐ míngtiān zěnme qù Běijīng?*  
女：坐飞机去。 *Zuò fēijī qù.*  
She says she will go by plane; B shows a plane.

**14. D**  
男：这个水果多少钱？ *Zhège shuǐguǒ duōshao qián?*  
女：五块钱。 *Wǔ kuài qián.*  
They are asking about the price of fruit in a shop; D shows a shop.

**15. E**  
女：你妈妈在哪儿工作？ *Nǐ māma zài nǎr gōngzuò?*  
男：她在医院工作。 *Tā zài yīyuàn gōngzuò.*  
He says his mother works in a hospital; E shows a hospital.

### Part 4 (16–20) — transcript

**16. A**  
我下午三点看电视。 *Wǒ xiàwǔ sān diǎn kàn diànshì.*  
问：他下午几点看电视？ *Tā xiàwǔ jǐ diǎn kàn diànshì?*  
A 三点 *sān diǎn*　B 四点 *sì diǎn*　C 五点 *wǔ diǎn*  
The statement says three o'clock; A is 三点.

**17. B**  
我坐飞机去北京。 *Wǒ zuò fēijī qù Běijīng.*  
问：他怎么去北京？ *Tā zěnme qù Běijīng?*  
A 坐火车 *zuò huǒchē*　B 坐飞机 *zuò fēijī*　C 坐出租车 *zuò chūzūchē*  
The speaker says “坐飞机”; B is 坐飞机.

**18. B**  
我爸爸是老师，他在学校工作。 *Wǒ bàba shì lǎoshī, tā zài xuéxiào gōngzuò.*  
问：他爸爸在哪儿工作？ *Tā bàba zài nǎr gōngzuò?*  
A 医院 *yīyuàn*　B 学校 *xuéxiào*　C 商店 *shāngdiàn*  
The statement says his father works at a school; B is 学校.

**19. C**  
我有一个女儿，她六岁。 *Wǒ yǒu yí gè nǚ'ér, tā liù suì.*  
问：他女儿几岁？ *Tā nǚ'ér jǐ suì?*  
A 五岁 *wǔ suì*　B 七岁 *qī suì*　C 六岁 *liù suì*  
The daughter is six years old; C is 六岁.

**20. A**  
今天天气很热。 *Jīntiān tiānqì hěn rè.*  
问：今天天气怎么样？ *Jīntiān tiānqì zěnmeyàng?*  
A 很热 *hěn rè*　B 很冷 *hěn lěng*　C 下雨 *xià yǔ*  
The statement says it is very hot; A is 很热.

## Reading

### Part 1 (21–25)

**21. ×** — 火车 *huǒchē*  
The picture shows a taxi (出租车), not a train (火车).

**22. √** — 猫 *māo*  
The picture shows a cat; 猫 means “cat”.

**23. ×** — 水 *shuǐ*  
The picture shows tea (茶), not a glass of water (水).

**24. √** — 水果 *shuǐguǒ*  
The picture shows fruit; 水果 means “fruit”.

**25. ×** — 电视 *diànshì*  
The picture shows a book (书), not a television (电视).

### Part 2 (26–30)

**26. A** — 我们中午在饭馆吃米饭。 *Wǒmen zhōngwǔ zài fànguǎn chī mǐfàn.*  
The sentence mentions eating in a restaurant; A shows a restaurant.

**27. B** — 他坐火车去北京。 *Tā zuò huǒchē qù Běijīng.*  
The sentence mentions a train; B shows a train station.

**28. C** — 她上午在商店买水果。 *Tā shàngwǔ zài shāngdiàn mǎi shuǐguǒ.*  
The sentence mentions a shop and fruit; C shows a shop.

**29. D** — 她上午在家看电视。 *Tā shàngwǔ zài jiā kàn diànshì.*  
The sentence mentions watching TV at home; D shows a television at home.

**30. E** — 我朋友是医生。 *Wǒ péngyou shì yīshēng.*  
The sentence says my friend is a doctor; E shows a doctor.

### Part 3 (31–35)

**31. A** — 你叫什么？ *Nǐ jiào shénme?*  
→ 我叫李月。 *Wǒ jiào Lǐ Yuè.*  
The question asks for a name; A gives the name Li Yue.

**32. D** — 你家有几个人？ *Nǐ jiā yǒu jǐ gè rén?*  
→ 我家有四个人。 *Wǒ jiā yǒu sì gè rén.*  
The question asks how many people are in the family; D says four.

**33. B** — 现在几点？ *Xiànzài jǐ diǎn?*  
→ 现在上午九点。 *Xiànzài shàngwǔ jiǔ diǎn.*  
The question asks the time; B says nine in the morning.

**34. F** — 你怎么去学校？ *Nǐ zěnme qù xuéxiào?*  
→ 我坐出租车去。 *Wǒ zuò chūzūchē qù.*  
The question asks how; F says “I go by taxi”.

**35. C** — 你吃什么水果？ *Nǐ chī shénme shuǐguǒ?*  
→ 我吃苹果。 *Wǒ chī píngguǒ.*  
The question asks what fruit you eat; C says “I eat apples”.

### Part 4 (36–40)

**36. A** — 你（　）在哪儿？  
→ 住 *zhù*  
“你住在哪儿？” asks where you live; A is 住.

**37. B** — 这个杯子（　）钱？  
→ 多少 *duōshao*  
“这个杯子多少钱？” asks the price; B is 多少.

**38. C** — 认识你，我很（　）。  
→ 高兴 *gāoxìng*  
“认识你，我很高兴” expresses pleasure; C is 高兴.

**39. F** — 女：（　），我不能去学校。 男：没关系。  
→ 对不起 *duìbuqǐ*  
The woman apologises, so 对不起 fits; F.

**40. D** — 你（　）王老师了吗？  
→ 看见 *kànjiàn*  
“你看见王老师了吗？” asks whether you saw Teacher Wang; D is 看见.

## Scoring

One mark for each correct answer; no mark for a wrong or blank answer. Listening has 20 marks and Reading has 20 marks. The raw practice score is **5 × correct answers in each section** out of 200, and the usual HSK 1 pass mark is 120/200. This conversion is for practice only; it is not an official equated HSK score and does not certify a level.
