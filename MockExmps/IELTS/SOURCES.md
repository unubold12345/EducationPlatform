# Format references and provenance

Consulted on **9 November 2026**. These official pages were used for structure and task conventions, not as sources of copied examination content.

- [IELTS Academic Listening format](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-academic-test/ielts-academic-format-listening): part structure, contextual progression and supported task types.
- [IELTS Academic Reading format](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-academic-test/ielts-academic-format-reading): text-length range, timing and question conventions.
- [IELTS Academic Writing format](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-academic-test/ielts-academic-format-writing): task requirements and assessment dimensions.
- [IELTS Academic Speaking format](https://ielts.org/take-a-test/test-types/ielts-academic-test/ielts-academic-format-speaking): interview stages, preparation and discussion conventions.

All passages, scripts, questions, alternatives, keys, explanations, diagrams and numerical data in this pack were authored for this task. Named study teams, towns, institutions and projects are fictional. They are not citations to published research. General expository descriptions serve the fictional teaching contexts; no empirical study results or specialist historical attributions are claimed.

The prior website's exams 01–06 were inspected only to avoid repeating their topics and to continue the numbering. These new files do not import or modify those definitions, recordings, answer keys or attempt snapshots. There are no official paper scans or copied third-party recordings in this pack.

The added Listening MP3s are original synthetic performances of the authored scripts using the locally installed Kokoro-82M ONNX model and stock English voices: `bf_emma`, `bf_isabella`, `bm_george`, `af_heart` and `am_michael`. The Apache-2.0 model and voice files were already present locally from earlier project audio work; no voice cloning, new media download or provider call was used. Package versions, exact model-file hashes, role assignments, text expansions, timestamps and output hashes are retained in the audio manifests. The model distribution used by the project is [Kokoro ONNX model-files-v1.0](https://github.com/thewh1teagle/kokoro-onnx/releases/tag/model-files-v1.0).

Authored by Codex (GPT-6), using PowerShell, Python and official-format web references. The local build is deterministic and has no provider/API calls.
