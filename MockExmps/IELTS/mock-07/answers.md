## Writing — content guidance, not model answers

### Task 1

An overview can contrast the net rise for under-18s and over-64s with the net decline for 18–64s. All groups fall between 2015 and 2020, then recover. Under-18 visits overtake 18–64 visits by 2015 and finish highest; the oldest group finishes close to working-age adults. Distinguish visits from unique people. Causes of the 2020 dip are not supplied.

### Task 2

Address near-term public needs and the possible value of uncertain fundamental work. Develop an actual position on priorities or balance. Examples can be hypothetical or drawn from experience; named studies are unnecessary. A balanced position needs a reasoned allocation principle, not merely a final sentence saying both are important.

Assess task achievement/response, coherence and cohesion, lexical resource, and grammatical range/accuracy. There is no single correct essay position. Do not reward agreement with these notes; reward a relevant developed response. For Task 1, use only the supplied visual; do not invent causes.
