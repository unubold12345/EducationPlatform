## Writing — content guidance, not model answers

### Task 1

Housing replaces food as the largest share. Housing and leisure shares rise; food and transport fall; other is unchanged as a share. Shares are not amounts: food rises from 360 to 400 units despite falling from 30% to 20%; transport rises from 240 to 300. Other rises from 120 to 200. Do not infer changes in purchasing power from nominal figures.

### Task 2

Develop both problems and measures. Individual costs can include time, fatigue and expense; community effects might include congestion or uneven demand. Match proposals to problems and consider practical limits. A response about the benefits of travel alone does not address the task.

Assess task achievement/response, coherence and cohesion, lexical resource, and grammatical range/accuracy. There is no single correct essay position. Do not reward agreement with these notes; reward a relevant developed response. For Task 1, use only the supplied visual; do not invent causes.
