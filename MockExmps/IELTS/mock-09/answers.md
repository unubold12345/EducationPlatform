## Writing — content guidance, not model answers

### Task 1

Self-catering capacity more than doubles, hotels grow more modestly and guesthouses contract. Guesthouse occupancy rises to the highest rate, while hotels decline slightly. Self-catering stays shorten but remain longest. Do not equate capacity with demand or add occupancy percentages across categories. Select comparisons rather than narrating all 18 values mechanically.

### Task 2

Discuss student choice and a common breadth of education. Consider motivation, future uncertainty and access to expertise. The position can favour one view or a justified combination, but should clarify what is compulsory and where choice belongs. Avoid switching to a general discussion of university specialisation.

Assess task achievement/response, coherence and cohesion, lexical resource, and grammatical range/accuracy. There is no single correct essay position. Do not reward agreement with these notes; reward a relevant developed response. For Task 1, use only the supplied visual; do not invent causes.
