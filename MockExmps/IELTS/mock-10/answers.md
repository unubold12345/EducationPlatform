## Writing — content guidance, not model answers

### Task 1

Give an overview of collection, preparation, manufacture and inspection, including the feedback loop. Distinguish rejected non-cork input leaving the process from failed cork panels returning to grinding/mixing. Mention binder input and heat/pressure without inventing numerical conditions. This is an authored proposed process, not a claim about every cork factory.

### Task 2

Evaluate flexible hours, rather than discussing only home working. Explain benefits and costs for workers or organisations and make a clear comparative judgement. Coordination, service coverage and caring responsibilities are relevant examples. A claim that there are advantages and disadvantages without weighing them leaves the main question unanswered.

Assess task achievement/response, coherence and cohesion, lexical resource, and grammatical range/accuracy. There is no single correct essay position. Do not reward agreement with these notes; reward a relevant developed response. For Task 1, use only the supplied visual; do not invent causes.
