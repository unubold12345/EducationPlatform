## Writing — content guidance, not model answers

### Task 1

Overview: the square becomes pedestrian-oriented, with greenery and a pavilion. The car park becomes a garden; the internal bus stop becomes café/seating, with the bus stop moved onto Main Road. Shops and toilets remain; the east–west road becomes a pedestrian path. Avoid claiming all transport is removed from the surrounding area or inferring relative building sizes from an unscaled plan.

### Task 2

Develop the value of intergenerational contact and of age-specific needs or interests. A combined approach can be argued, with examples of which activities suit each arrangement. Do not assume all members of an age group have identical preferences. State and support the opinion throughout the response.

Assess task achievement/response, coherence and cohesion, lexical resource, and grammatical range/accuracy. There is no single correct essay position. Do not reward agreement with these notes; reward a relevant developed response. For Task 1, use only the supplied visual; do not invent causes.
