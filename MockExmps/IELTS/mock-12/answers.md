## Writing — content guidance, not model answers

### Task 1

Online methods grow strongly; printed self-study and in-person participation fall. Recorded courses become most used, while in-person classes have the greatest weekly hours among users. Live online quadruples its share. Do not sum overlapping respondents, treat hours as total regional hours, or infer that every adult abandons one method for another.

### Task 2

Take a reasoned overall position while acknowledging relevant limitations. Discuss rapid information sharing, accountability or choice alongside credibility, manipulation or unfair judgement. The task concerns the ability to publish immediate reviews, not social media in general. Examples do not need to identify real companies or individuals.

Assess task achievement/response, coherence and cohesion, lexical resource, and grammatical range/accuracy. There is no single correct essay position. Do not reward agreement with these notes; reward a relevant developed response. For Task 1, use only the supplied visual; do not invent causes.
