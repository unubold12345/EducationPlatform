# Reader-administered mock protocol

These are original offline drafts. Use a candidate booklet and blank answer sheet. Keep `answers.md`, `mock.json` and `examiner-listening.md` with the examiner. Use separate section timers; a learner may not return to an earlier section. Breaks between sections are optional and excluded from the stated test times.

| Level | Vocabulary | Grammar + reading | Listening cap |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| N5 | 20 min | 40 min | 30 min |
| N4 | 25 min | 55 min | 35 min |

## Written sections

Give the booklet only after explaining the section timing. Select one answer per question. 【 】 marks the word under test in vocabulary. In sentence composition, arrange all four chunks, then mark the option containing the **third** chunk, at ★. The option numbers identify the shuffled chunks, not their final positions. Text-grammar blanks 【1】–【4】 share one passage. Questions under a shared reading passage use the same passage, not a new context.

At the end of each section, collect or cover its answer sheet before starting the next. Use dictionaries, answer explanations, transcript reading and replay only in a later review session.

## Listening

A fluent Japanese reader is required. There is no recording, TTS model, audio licence or measured track duration in this pack. Use the section time as a maximum administration window; do not claim that a short reader session has reproduced official recorded pacing. Do not add a long silent wait just to reach the cap. For the most consistent future timing, record a rehearsal with the actual reader and review intelligibility before making a timed recording.

1. Allow about one minute to explain the section and locate the answer rows. At a group transition, explain its task without translating the Japanese stimulus or revealing an answer.
2. **Task comprehension:** allow about 10 seconds to preview the printed alternatives. Read the introductory cue, then the dialogue/announcement, then the question once at the end. **Key points:** read the question first, allow about 10 seconds to preview the printed alternatives, then read the dialogue/announcement and repeat the question. Follow `examinerReadout` in each case. The candidate booklet does not print these spoken questions. Allow about 10–15 seconds for an answer. Typical item windows are roughly 45–75 seconds, depending on dialogue length and level.
3. **Verbal expressions:** allow about 5–10 seconds for the written situation cue. Read the scene narration, then the three numbered spoken alternatives in their generated order. The candidate sees only the scene cue and numbers 1–3. Allow about 10 seconds to answer.
4. **Quick response:** there is no preview of the stimulus or alternatives. Read the stimulus once and the three numbered responses, with a brief pause between alternatives. Allow about 5–8 seconds to answer. Do not repeat the stimulus.
5. Read naturally at a clear beginner pace for N5, and a slightly more natural everyday pace for N4. Distinguish speaker turns without announcing the authoring labels 男, 女 or ナレーター. Do not say the parenthetical kanji readings twice. Do not read explanations, answer keys, headings or timing notes.
6. Do not translate, improvise clues, correct a candidate or repeat a misunderstood item during the mock. If the reader makes a substantive reading error, log the affected question and exclude it from the raw denominator rather than pretending the delivery was valid.

The full listening scripts are intentionally also usable for untimed study. Text-only situation cues and alternatives are documented adaptations, and the scripts have not been checked through recorded playback. They do not reproduce the full acoustic or visual demands of the official exam.

## Scoring and review

Mark with that form's `answers.md`. Every item is worth one raw practice mark. Report, for example, `Vocabulary 16/21; Grammar + reading 15/22; Listening 18/24; Total 49/67`. This example demonstrates notation only; it is not a learner result or a pass prediction. When an item is excluded because of reader error, state the changed denominator.

For sentence-order review, show the complete sentence from the key. Review each listening error with the private transcript after marking. All forms remain drafts pending Japanese-teacher review; an ambiguity identified in review should be corrected in the source and the pack rebuilt before reuse.
