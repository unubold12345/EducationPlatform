# Format references

The forms in this pack are original. These sources were read on 2026-11-18 only to establish the test format; no
question, passage, script, picture or recording was copied from them or from any ETS material.

## Official

- ETS, *TOEFL iBT Test: 2026 Update - Test Blueprint and Specifications Document*
  (<https://www.in.ets.org/content/dam/ets-org/pdfs/toefl/toefl-ibt-test-specifications-2026.pdf>). Taken from it:
  the four sections and twelve task types; items per task type (Complete the Words 30, Read in Daily Life 5-15,
  Read an Academic Passage 5-15; Choose a Response 15-19, Conversation 10, Announcement 6-10, Academic Talk 8-16;
  Build a Sentence 10, Email 1, Academic Discussion 1; Listen and Repeat 7, Interview 4); totals of 50, 47, 12 and
  11 items; raw points 35, 35, 20 and 55; router and module times (Reading 18-21 + 9 min, Listening 18 + 7 or 11
  min, Writing 23 min, Speaking 8 min); two-stage adaptive Reading and Listening; text lengths (15-50 words for
  simple texts, up to 200 for academic passages; audio from one short utterance to 250 words); accents (US/Canadian,
  British, Australian); 1 point per Reading, Listening and Build a Sentence item, 5 per constructed response.

## Unofficial descriptions of the on-screen format

Used where the blueprint is silent. Where they disagree, the blueprint wins.

- TOEFL Resources, reading guide (<https://www.toeflresources.com/toefl-reading/>): Module 1 of about 35
  questions and Module 2 of about 15; Complete the Words paragraphs of about 70 words with ten truncated words
  in the second and third sentences and intact first and last sentences; daily-life texts with two or three
  questions; 200-word passages with five questions; question types.
- TOEFL Resources, writing guide (<https://www.toeflresources.com/writing-section/>): Build a Sentence layout
  (context line, lowercase boxes, multi-word boxes, occasional unused box); the email and discussion prompts and
  times.
- TOEFL Resources, speaking guide (<https://www.toeflresources.com/speaking-section/>): the Listen and Repeat
  scenario line and syllable ranges; the interview introduction, the four-question progression, 45 seconds, no
  preparation.
- Test Ninjas listening overview (<https://test-ninjas.com/toefl-listening>) and Magoosh listening overview
  (<https://magoosh.com/toefl/toefl-listening/>): four printed replies in Choose a Response; two questions per
  conversation and announcement, four per talk; audio lengths.
- GoArno (<https://goarno.io/blog/listen-and-repeat-guide-toefl/>): Listen and Repeat response windows of 8, 10
  and 12 seconds.

## Decisions made by this pack

- **Module split.** ETS publishes totals per path, not per module. Module 1 = 35 Reading and 32 Listening items,
  Module 2 = 15 items; in Listening this is the only split with whole sets that gives both published paths
  (Lower 19/10/10/8 and Upper 15/10/6/16).
- **Routing at about 60 %** (21 of 35, 20 of 32). ETS's thresholds are not public.
- **Build a Sentence in 6 minutes**, so that the three writing tasks add up to the blueprint's 23 minutes; some
  guides report 6 minutes 50 seconds.
- **Every item is scored.** The real forms carry unscored trial items that cannot be identified.
- **Passage questions are all four-option multiple choice.** The on-screen test also has a click-a-sentence type,
  which a printed paper cannot reproduce.
