# Sources for the TOPIK pack

Only the paper's structure was taken from these sources: sections, timing, question order, task types, printed
instructions, paired sets, per-question points and level cut scores. No item, script, text, picture or answer
from a real paper is reproduced. Every dialogue, passage, chart, option, picture and explanation in
`topik-i/` and `topik-ii/` is new. Checked 2026-11-17/18.

| --- | --- |

Study sites (topikguide.com, learning-korean.com, info.topiklab.com) were read only to cross-check the task
order; their own mock items were not used.

Not official: the forms are not equated, so the level shown in results is a practice estimate. TOPIK Ⅱ writing is
not graded on the site (the learner scores it with the rubric and model answer).
